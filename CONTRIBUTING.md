# Contributing to Project Hyena

Thank you for your interest in Project Hyena!

The project is still in an early alpha stage, so accepting outside contributions is not our main focus right now. That said, we will never turn away anyone who wants to help. If you would like to contribute, you are welcome to, and your help is truly appreciated.

Formal contribution guidelines will be added here as the project grows. Until then, please keep changes small and clearly describe what they change and why.

Project Hyena is based on the Firestorm Viewer and the Second Life Viewer and is released under the LGPL license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
